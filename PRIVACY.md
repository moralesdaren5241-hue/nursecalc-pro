# NurseCalc Pro — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026
**Applies to:** the NurseCalc Pro Android app (package `com.nursecalcpro.app`) and the NurseCalc Pro web app.

## Summary

NurseCalc Pro does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or health data. All calculations run on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK.

## What the app stores, and where

NurseCalc Pro keeps a small amount of data **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

| Stored on your device | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Display settings (theme, text size, reduce motion) | Remember how you like the app to look |
| Unit and precision settings (default weight unit, infusion-pump precision) | Remember your defaults |
| Optional greeting name, if you type one | Show “Good morning, …” on the home screen |
| Favourite calculators, Quick-access list, recently opened calculator names, bookmarked and recently viewed formula references | Navigation shortcuts |
| Study progress (questions answered, correct answers, streak, missed-question list) | Track your practice |

**Not stored:** the numbers you type into calculators, calculation results, fluid-log entries and sequential-infusion plans are held in memory only while the screen is open and are discarded when you leave it. They are never written to storage and never leave your device.

This data is **not** included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer (backup is disabled for the app).

## What the app sends over the network

Nothing. The Android app contains the complete application and does not request the Android internet permission. It makes no network requests.

The only network activity happens if **you** tap the “Read the full privacy policy” link in the app: your device's browser then opens this policy on github.com, which is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

**Web version:** if you use NurseCalc Pro in a web browser, the page is delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard request data (such as your IP address) to deliver the page; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). After the page has loaded, the web app works the same way as the Android app and sends nothing.

## Patient information

NurseCalc Pro is not designed to hold patient information and has no field for patient identifiers. Do not enter patient names or other identifying details anywhere in the app (including the optional greeting name).

## Third parties

No data is shared with, sold to, or received from any third party. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting libraries.

## Deleting your data

- In the app: **Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all local data**.
- Or uninstall the app (Android removes all of its stored data), or clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → NurseCalc Pro → Storage.
- Web version: clear the site data for the page in your browser.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held by the developer to delete.

## Children

NurseCalc Pro is intended for nursing students and nurses. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Because no data is transmitted, there is no data in transit to protect. Data stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's application sandbox.

## Medical disclaimer

NurseCalc Pro is an educational and calculation aid. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and does not replace professional judgement, the prescription, product labelling or institutional protocols.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. The version history of this document is public in the project repository.
